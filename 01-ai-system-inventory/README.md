# AI System Inventory & Classification

## Case Study

**Organisation:** OrbitTel Communications

**Sector:** Telecommunications

## Scenario

OrbitTel Communications is a fictional telecommunications company operating across mobile, fiber broadband, customer operations, network operations, and enterprise services.

## Objective

Create a structured inventory of OrbitTel's AI systems, classify each system according to applicable AI governance and regulatory considerations, and identify initial governance gaps using the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

## What This Project Demonstrates

- Ability to structure an AI system inventory for a telecommunications organisation
- Understanding of risk-based AI governance and proportionality
- Ability to apply regulatory classification logic to realistic AI use cases
- Practical application of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- Ability to identify initial governance gaps and ownership requirements

 ## Frameworks Applied

- EU AI Act
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
- ISO/IEC 42001

## Artifacts

- AI System Inventory
- EU AI Act Classification
- NIST AI RMF Mapping

 ## Key Governance Questions

- What AI systems exist across the organisation?
- Who owns each AI system?
- What is each system used for?
- Who is affected by its outputs?
- What level of governance is required?
- What initial governance gaps need to be addressed?
