# NIST AI RMF Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps OrbitTel Communications' AI systems to the four core functions of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework:

- Govern
- Map
- Measure
- Manage

The mapping provides an initial view of governance gaps. It is not a detailed AI risk assessment.

---

## System-001: Customer Churn Prediction

### Govern

**Current State:** System owner identified.

**Governance Gap:** No documented AI governance process or formal accountability structure is currently defined.

### Map

**Current State:** Purpose, business function, owner, data type, and affected parties are documented.

**Governance Gap:** The system's potential impact on customer treatment requires further assessment.

### Measure

**Governance Gap:** No documented evidence of model accuracy, fairness testing, or performance monitoring.

### Manage

**Governance Gap:** No documented process for responding to inaccurate predictions or unintended customer impacts.

## Cross-System Governance Gaps

The initial mapping identifies several common governance gaps across OrbitTel's AI inventory:

1. Formal AI ownership and accountability
2. Documented AI governance policies and procedures
3. Model performance monitoring
4. Fairness and bias evaluation where relevant
5. Human oversight
6. AI incident and escalation processes
7. Evidence and documentation
8. Ongoing monitoring and review

## Conclusion

The NIST AI RMF mapping provides an initial governance view of OrbitTel's AI inventory.

The identified gaps should be addressed through future governance activities, including detailed risk assessment, control definition, evidence collection, monitoring, and incident management.
