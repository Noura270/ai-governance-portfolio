# Project 3: Responsible AI Policy & Operating Model

## Scenario
- **Organization:** OrbitTel Telecommunications
- **Context:** Following the system inventory classification (Project 1) and deep-dive risk assessment of the Fiber Network Fault Prediction Engine `OT-AI-003` (Project 2), OrbitTel's Executive Board has authorized the formal rollout of the **Enterprise AI Governance Programme**. 
- **Objective:** The AI Governance Programme Office (AGPO) has developed two foundational pillars: a comprehensive **Responsible AI Policy** defining corporate commitments, and an **AI Governance Operating Model** detailing the exact execution workflows, approval gates, and organizational accountabilities.

---

## What This Project Demonstrates
- **Operationalization of Principles:** Translating ethical principles (fairness, explainability, human oversight) into clear operational gates rather than high-level statements.
- **Enterprise Governance Structure:** Designing multi-tiered organizational oversight (Board, AI Governance Committee, Programme Office, System Owners).
- **Practical Alignment with Global Standards:** Implementing the **NIST AI RMF (Govern Function)**, **EU AI Act (Articles 9, 14, 28–29)**, and **ISO/IEC 42001** management standards.
- **Proportionate Review Workflows:** Balancing risk oversight with operational efficiency through tiered intake (Standard vs. Enhanced Review).

## Core Design Principles
1. **Principles into Process:** Every ethical commitment directly links to a mandatory checkpoint in the review process.
2. **Proportionate Governance:** Minimal/Limited-risk systems move through accelerated Standard Review; High-risk systems require rigorous Enhanced Review.
3. **Clear Accountability:** Every AI system in production must have a single named **System Owner** accepting governance responsibility.

---

## Frameworks Applied
- **NIST AI RMF:** Govern Function (Culture, Policies, Roles, Systems)
- **EU AI Act:** Article 9 (Risk Management System), Article 14 (Human Oversight), Article 28–29 (Deployer Obligations)
- **ISO/IEC 42001:** AI Management System Requirements
