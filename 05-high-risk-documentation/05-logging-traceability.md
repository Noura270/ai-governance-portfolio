# Document 5: Logging and Traceability

**System:** Network Care & Fraud Shield Pro (`OT-AI-001`)  
**EU AI Act Reference:** Article 12 (Record-Keeping)  
**Document Type:** Provider (NetFlow) + Deployer (OrbitTel)  
**Version:** 1.0 — March 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose
Article 12 of the EU AI Act mandates that high-risk AI systems maintain continuous, automated logging capabilities throughout their operational lifecycle. These logs must enable post-hoc verification, anomaly reconstruction, traceability of system outputs, and evidence collection for regulatory compliance audits (e.g., telecom regulators, ACM, and AI authorities).

---

## 2. Mandatory Logging Events Matrix

To satisfy Article 12 and support post-incident investigations, `OT-AI-001` records the following data points for every execution:

| Category | Logged Events & Parameters | Purpose & Regulatory Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Input Telemetry** | Cell tower node ID, traffic volume, protocol headers, SIM event frequency, ingestion timestamp | Enables full reconstruction of the raw operational context evaluated by the AI. |
| **Model Inferences** | Fraud risk score, dynamic throttle recommendation, confidence interval, model version (`v3.1.2`), inference timestamp | Records precisely what the AI recommended and the statistical basis for the score. |
| **Operator Actions** | Reviewer ID, review start/end timestamp, decision (accept/modify/override), minimum 20-character rationale note | Establishes human oversight accountability and tracks potential automation bias. |
| **System Exceptions** | Telemetry ingestion drops, low-confidence score flags ($< 75\%$), special data filter triggers | Supports rapid technical troubleshooting and system stability monitoring. |
| **System Configuration** | Policy updates, threshold changes, user access logins/logouts | Maintains an immutable record of system environment changes. |

## 3. Logging Architecture & Access Controls

### 3.1 Provider Logs (NetFlow Technologies Ltd)
- **Scope:** Model API execution logs, neural network weight hashes, and base infrastructure telemetry.
- **Retention:** 36 months in secure, geographically redundant EU cloud storage.
- **Access SLA:** Available to OrbitTel within 24 hours for emergency incident response, 5 business days for standard compliance audits.

### 3.2 Deployer Logs (OrbitTel Infrastructure)
- **Scope:** Full operational chain including raw ingested telemetry, NOC engineer review logs, override rationales, and network rule execution commands.
- **Storage Location:** OrbitTel secure EU Data Lake integrated with the enterprise Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system.
- **Retention Period:** **36 Months** (aligned with EU AI Act Article 12(1) and Dutch/EU telecom regulatory dispute limitation windows).

### 5. Log Security, Integrity, and Confidentiality

To prevent log tampering, unauthorized modification, or data leakage, OrbitTel enforces the following technical controls:
* **Immutability:** Logs are written to Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) append-only storage.
* **Cryptographic Hashing:** Critical log entries (inferences and operator decisions) are chained using SHA-256 cryptographic hashing.
* **Encryption:** All log archives are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
* **Access Control:** Strictly restricted via Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Only authorized AGPO auditors and InfoSec lead personnel hold read access.

---

### 6. Regulatory Access & Audit Readiness

In compliance with Article 12(2), all logs generated by `OT-AI-001` are structured in a machine-readable format (JSON-LD) capable of being exported and submitted to national supervisory authorities (e.g., Dutch Authority for Digital Infrastructure - RDI / ACM) within **24 hours** of a formal regulatory request.
