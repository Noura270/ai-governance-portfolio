# Project 4: AI Incident Response & Regulatory Escalation Scenario

## Scenario Overview
- **Organization:** OrbitTel Telecommunications
- **Incident ID:** `AI-INC-2026-001`
- **System Affected:** `OT-AI-001` (Predictive Network Optimization & Anti-Fraud Engine)
- **Incident Summary:** Systematically throttling bandwidth and incorrectly flagging high-risk fraud alerts for subscribers residing in specific regional postcodes/telecom zones, correlating with protected demographic characteristics and lower-income districts.
- **Detection Date:** March 2026 (Two months after OrbitTel's enterprise AI governance framework deployment).

---

## What This Project Demonstrates
1. **Continuous AI Governance:** Operationalization of AI governance during live operational failures rather than static pre-deployment audits.
2. **Structured Incident Response:** End-to-end management spanning Detection, Containment, Investigation, Remediation, and Closure.
3. **Regulatory Escalation Protocols:** Alignment with notification requirements under EU AI Act (Article 73 - Serious Incidents), GDPR Article 22, and Telecom Regulatory Authorities (NTRA/BEREC equivalents).
4. **NIST AI RMF (MANAGE Function):** Operational application of incident management, risk containment, and corrective feedback loops.
5. **Root Cause Analysis (RCA):** Deployment of the 5 Whys methodology to trace algorithmic failures to underlying governance and dataset gaps.

## Frameworks & Standards Applied
- **NIST AI RMF:** MANAGE Function (Subcategories 1.1, 2.1, 2.2, 4.1).
- **EU AI Act:** Article 9 (Risk Management), Article 12 (Logging & Traceability), Article 17 (Post-market Monitoring), Article 73 (Serious Incident Reporting).
- **ISO/IEC 27035:** Information security incident management adapted for AI/ML lifecycle risks.
- **GDPR:** Article 22 (Automated Decision-Making & Human Intervention Rights).
