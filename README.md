## 🚀 Projects Overview

### Project 1: OrbitTel `OT-AI-001` High-Risk AI Compliance Pack
* **Description:** A comprehensive regulatory compliance and risk governance package for a high-risk AI telecommunications system (Network Care & Fraud Shield Pro), aligned with the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF.
* **Key Components:**
  - `01-ai-system-inventory`: System intake and profiling questionnaire (EU AI Act Annex IV).
  - `02-ai-risk-assessment`: Core risk assessment and scoring methodology for the telecommunications AI model.
  - `03-ai-governance-policy`: Governance operating model, roles, and responsibilities.
  - `03-ai-governance-policy`: Governance operating model, roles, and responsibilities.
  - `04-incident-response`: Root-cause analysis and incident mitigation playbooks.
  - `05-high-risk-documentation`: Complete high-risk AI documentation pack and technical artefacts.

### Project 2: Responsible AI Policy Auditor (Applied App)
* **Description:** A working, evidence-based AI governance application that evaluates AI systems and prompts against multiple global frameworks simultaneously (OWASP, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, and GDPR).
* **Key Features:**
  - Two-stage pipeline separating risk scoring ($Likelihood \times Impact$) from evidence-based compliance checks.
  - Multi-risk detection across 11 distinct risk categories.
  - SQLite audit logs and ChromaDB policy knowledge base.
  - Interactive Streamlit dashboard and reporting.
* **Repository:** [responsible-ai-policy-auditor](https://github.com/Noura270/responsible-ai-policy-auditor)
